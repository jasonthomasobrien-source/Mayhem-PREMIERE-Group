Leaderboard Row Elements

Which combination feels most encouraging and attractive?

A

Streaks + Pace Indicator

Shows: Rank, name, attempts, leads, conversion %, 🔥 streak, on-pace status

Pros

  • Encourages daily consistency
  • Clear visual cue if falling behind
  • Celebrates momentum

Cons

  • Busier rows
  • More visual elements to parse
B

Streaks Only (Minimal)

Shows: Rank, name, attempts, leads, conversion %, 🔥 streak

Pros

  • Clean, refined look
  • Streak is the main action motivator
  • Easier to scan

Cons

  • No clear pace feedback
  • Less urgency for those falling behind
C

Streaks + Conversion Leader + Pace

Shows: Everything above, plus special badges (conversion king, MVP, pace threats)

Pros

  • Multiple ways to compete & win
  • Recognizes different achievements
  • Most gamified feel

Cons

  • Visually dense
  • Risk of looking cluttered
