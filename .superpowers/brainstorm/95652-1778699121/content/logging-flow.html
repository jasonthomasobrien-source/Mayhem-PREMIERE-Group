Logging Flow & Quick Buttons

Two questions: (1) Do you want the quick-tap buttons at all? (2) If yes, what numbers/labels feel right?

A

No Quick Buttons

Single "Log Activity" button opens the modal. Users choose single-day or range, enter amounts. Minimal, clean.

  • Simplest interface
  • All logging flows through one dialog
  • No decision paralysis on home page
  • Feels more intentional
B

Single Quick Button: +5

"Log Activity" button + one quick tap for "Log 5 outreach today" (the daily goal). Everything else via modal.

  • Balances speed + simplicity
  • 5 is the daily target, so it's natural
  • Covers 80% of quick logs
  • No clutter
C

Custom Quick Buttons

Suggest which numbers resonate: +3, +5, +10? Or something else entirely (e.g., +5, +15, +25 for weekly chunks)?

  • Speed for common cases
  • Lets us find the right numbers
  • More flexible than just +5

Note: The modal will work like this:

  • Log Today's Activity: Pick today's date (pre-filled), enter attempts + leads, submit
  • Log for a Range: Pick start date + end date, enter total attempts + leads for that span, submit
  • No per-day math needed in range mode — just total across the range
